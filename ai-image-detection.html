Technical notes

What the project is designed to investigate

Whether an image classifier can learn signals that distinguish real photographs from AI-generated content, and how data preparation and evaluation choices affect performance and generalization.

The preprocessing stage was a collaborative responsibility. Detailed model architectures, empirical metrics and representative outputs will be added only once documented and verified.
